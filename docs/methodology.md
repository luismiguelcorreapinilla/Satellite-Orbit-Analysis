# Methodology

The project follows a reproducible workflow composed of four stages.

## Stage 1 — TLE Acquisition

Current orbital information is retrieved from public TLE catalogs.

↓

## Stage 2 — Orbital Elements

The orbital parameters are extracted and interpreted.

↓

## Stage 3 — Orbit Propagation

Satellite trajectories are propagated using orbital mechanics models.

↓

## Stage 4 — Coverage Analysis

Ground tracks are intersected with Colombian cities to evaluate satellite visibility and revisit opportunities.

The complete workflow is implemented through Jupyter notebooks and integrated into an interactive Streamlit application.
