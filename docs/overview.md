# Satellite Orbit Analysis over Colombia

## Overview

Satellite Orbit Analysis over Colombia is a scientific workflow developed by GeoSpatial Intelligence Lab for analyzing the trajectories and spatial coverage of Earth observation satellites over Colombian territory.

The project integrates orbital mechanics, satellite propagation models, and geospatial visualization techniques to reconstruct satellite ground tracks from publicly available Two-Line Element (TLE) data.

Through interactive visualization and spatial analysis, the workflow enables users to evaluate satellite visibility, revisit frequency, and orbital coverage over major Colombian cities.

## Main Capabilities

- Download and process TLE data.
- Propagate satellite orbits.
- Visualize ground tracks.
- Analyze satellite coverage.
- Explore results through an interactive dashboard.

## Applications

- Earth Observation
- Remote Sensing
- Satellite Mission Planning
- Space Education
- Geospatial Intelligence
- Environmental Monitoring
